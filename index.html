How we work together

  1. Tell me the idea

    Send your footage with WeTransfer or a Google Drive link, plus your full script and a short note on the style you want.

  2. First cut

    I send a rough cut so you can see the direction early.

  3. Revisions

    2 rounds of changes are included. You share feedback and I polish the edit until it’s right.

  4. Final delivery

    You get the final file in the format and size you need.
